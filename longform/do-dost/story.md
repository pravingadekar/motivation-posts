# दो दोस्त, एक जैसी शुरुआत: 15 साल बाद एक खुश था, दूसरा नहीं

Long-form YouTube story (16:9, 30-40 min) for Motivational guru. A fictional story, never called "asli" or "sachchi kahani".
Theme: small daily habits, not money. No money promises, no real people, no religion or politics.

## Characters (keep the same look in every image)
- **कुणाल (Kunal):** slim, neat side-parted black hair, round gentle face, calm smile. Child: light blue school shirt. Adult (33): short trimmed beard, simple white shirt, a small brown diary.
- **अमित (Amit):** messy spiky black hair, sharper face, confident grin. Child: red t-shirt. Adult (33): styled hair, dark blazer, expensive watch, tired eyes.
- **दादाजी (Kunal's grandfather):** white dhoti-kurta, thick round glasses, white moustache, an old leather diary.

## Chapters (~6-8 min each)
1. **एक जैसी शुरुआत** — cold open: the two meet at the station years later, one calm, one tired. Flashback to childhood: same lane, same school bench. Amit is told he is "smart" and stops trying; Kunal writes one thing a night in a notebook. Dadaji's line: "बड़ा फ़र्क़ बड़े फ़ैसलों से नहीं, रोज़ की छोटी आदतों से बनता है।" Both leave for college on the same train; Dadaji gives Kunal an empty diary.
2. **आज़ादी की पहली आदत** — city college. Amit: late nights, phone till 3 a.m., skipping class, comparing himself with richer friends. Kunal: morning walk, ten pages a day, calls his mother every Sunday, a small tutoring job.
3. **पहली हार** — both fail at something big (a job interview / a project). Amit blames everyone and hides. Kunal writes what went wrong and tries again in small steps.
4. **रिश्ते और सेहत** — Amit's anger and pride push people away; health slips. Kunal learns to say sorry first, keeps promises, sleeps on time. A crisis at home tests both.
5. **स्टेशन पर वो रात** — back to the cold open. Amit asks "what did you do differently?" Kunal opens the diary: the 7 small habits. Amit realises it is not too late; the story ends with him starting with one habit. CTA: comment the one habit you will start today, share with a friend.

## Kunal's 7 habits (revealed in chapter 5)
1. सोने से पहले दिन की एक सीख लिखना
2. सुबह फ़ोन से पहले 10 मिनट खुद के साथ
3. रोज़ 10 पन्ने पढ़ना
4. ग़लती पर पहले "सॉरी" बोलना
5. हर हफ़्ते घर पर एक फ़ोन
6. दूसरों से नहीं, कल वाले खुद से तुलना
7. जो वादा किया, छोटा ही सही, पूरा करना
